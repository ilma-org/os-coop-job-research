# Position and job description

Assignment Appendix C. What a Computer Science student should understand and practice before applying.

## Scope

- Job title and typical job description
- Major duties and responsibilities
- Typical daily or weekly tasks (including on-call and incident work)
- Types of projects that may be assigned
- Educational background normally expected
- Experience, portfolio, certificates or other qualifications that help
- Hardware, operating systems, software and tools commonly associated with the position
