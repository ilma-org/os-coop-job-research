# Internships and job opportunities at Google

## Summary

The SRE books mention interns only in two case studies: a Google SRE team that expected three interns to help its workload, and the Chrome security team, which has converted interns to full-time employees. They also describe internal routes into and out of SRE: the six-month Mission Control rotation for product developers, and transfer to product development for SREs who passed the software engineer hiring bar. The books do not describe an SRE internship program, its eligibility, locations or how to apply, so this needs another source such as Google's careers pages.

## Key points

- Interns have joined Google teams: one SRE team expected three interns to improve its workload, and the Chrome security team has converted interns to full-time employees. (12-32, 12-33)
- Mission Control lets product development engineers spend six months embedded in an SRE team. (12-30)
- SREs who passed the software engineer hiring bar can transfer to product development teams. (12-31)
