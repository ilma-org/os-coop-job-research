# Internships and job opportunities at Google

## Summary

The SRE books mention interns only in two case studies: a Google SRE team that expected three interns to help its workload, and the Chrome security team, which has converted interns to full-time employees. They also describe internal routes into and out of SRE: the six-month Mission Control rotation for product developers, and transfer to product development for SREs who passed the software engineer hiring bar. The books do not describe an SRE internship program, so the details come from Google's careers site: its 2027 posting for a Software Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering BS/MS Intern in London states the eligibility (penultimate or final-year students in the EMEA region), the duration, the deadline, that there is no immigration sponsorship, the application materials and the SRE intern's work. Treat these as the terms of one posting, not a standing program.

## Key points

- Interns have joined Google teams: one SRE team expected three interns to improve its workload, and the Chrome security team has converted interns to full-time employees. (12-32, 12-33)
- Mission Control lets product development engineers spend six months embedded in an SRE team. (12-30)
- SREs who passed the software engineer hiring bar can transfer to product development teams. (12-31)
- Google's careers site has a Students section with Internships, Early career, Apprenticeships and Build your future. (12-48)
- The 2027 London posting offers internships in either Software Engineering or Site-Reliability Engineering. (12-35)
- Eligibility: students in their penultimate or final year of a full-time Bachelors or Masters degree in the EMEA region, located in the UK for the program. (12-36)
- Timing: at least 13 weeks full-time over Summer 2027; apply before 23 October 2026; applicants are not eligible for immigration sponsorship. (12-37, 12-38, 12-40)
- To apply: an updated CV or resume and a current transcript in English. (12-42)
- The SRE intern works on projects such as troubleshooting traffic anomalies, monitoring and alerts and building automation infrastructure; Unix/Linux is among the preferred experience. (12-44, 12-46)
