\paragraph{Background and services.}
Google's SRE book says that SREs at Google operate services such as planet-scale storage, email for hundreds of millions of users and web search, which is where Google began \cite{sre-book}. The ``site'' in SRE first meant keeping the google.com website running, and SRE now also runs services that are not websites, from internal infrastructure such as Bigtable to Google Cloud Platform for external developers \cite{sre-book}. The book names Search, Ads, Gmail and Photos as services whose reliability targets Google analyses \cite{sre-book}. SRE itself started when the book's author, who joined in 2003, was tasked with running a ``Production Team'' of seven engineers \cite{sre-book}. Google's own company page says the Google story begins in 1995 at Stanford University, that Google Inc.\ was officially born in August 1998 after Sun co-founder Andy Bechtolsheim wrote a check for \$100,000, and that Google has since moved to its current headquarters, the Googleplex, in Mountain View, California \cite{about-our-story}. It says Google makes hundreds of products used by billions of people \cite{about-our-story}. Alphabet's June 2026 investor presentation says annual revenue more than doubled from 2020 to 2025, to over \$400 billion, and that 13 products have more than a billion users, five of them more than 3 billion \cite{blog-alphabet-investor-presentation-june-2026}. The only customer count we found is a May 2023 Google Workspace post that gives 3 billion users and 9 million paying customers for Workspace alone \cite{workspace-google-blog-product-announcements-workflows-across-workspace}.

\paragraph{How SRE is organized.}
SRE and product development are separate organizations at Google, each with its own focus, priorities and management \cite{workbook}. Product teams own their service by default, and SRE is not available or warranted for most services \cite{workbook}. Product teams fund the growth of SRE with new hires when a product succeeds \cite{workbook}. SRE has infrastructural teams, service teams and horizontal product teams \cite{sre-book}. It is not a command-and-control organization, and because of emergency response and pager rotations it is spread over at least a few time zones \cite{sre-book}. Google has hundreds of on-call rotations, and many developer teams, not only SRE, are on call for their own service \cite{workbook}. The Workbook also says the efficacy of SRE at Google is tied to Google's widely accessible unified codebase \cite{workbook}. The books give neither the size of the SRE organization nor its office locations.

\paragraph{Working environment and culture.}
SRE at Google inherited much of its culture from the surrounding company \cite{workbook}. Google says it assumes failure can happen at any time, and that it does not get its culture right all the time \cite{bsrs}. Google prefers multi-site teams in part because night shifts harm people's health \cite{sre-book}, and it compensates on-call work with time off or cash, capped at a proportion of overall salary \cite{workbook}. All new SREs attend SRE EDU, a weeklong course on key concepts, tooling and platforms, and can later spend a week with another team through SRE Exchange \cite{workbook}. The books do not describe offices, working hours, remote-work policy or pay beyond on-call compensation.

\paragraph{Opportunities for students.}
The books mention interns only in two case studies: an SRE team that expected three interns to help its workload \cite{workbook}, and the Chrome security team, which has converted interns to full-time employees \cite{bsrs}. Engineers from product development can spend six months in an SRE team through the Mission Control program \cite{workbook}. Google's careers site has a Students section with internships, early-career roles and apprenticeships \cite{google-about-careers-applications-students}. Its 2027 posting for a Software Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering BS/MS Intern in London is the SRE internship we found \cite{\internad}. It is intended for students in their penultimate or final year of a full-time Bachelors or Masters degree in the EMEA region, who must be located in the UK for the program \cite{\internad}. The internship lasts at least 13 weeks in summer 2027, applications close on 23 October 2026, and Google says applicants are not eligible for immigration sponsorship \cite{\internad}. Applicants need a CV and a transcript in English \cite{\internad}. The Site Reliability intern works on projects such as troubleshooting traffic anomalies, monitoring and alerts, and building automation, and Unix/Linux is among the preferred experience \cite{\internad}. In our reading, a student in Thailand falls outside the EMEA condition of this posting. We found no SRE internship posting open to students in our region.
